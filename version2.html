Lead capture & follow-up

Every enquiry gets a fast reply and lands in your pipeline — no copy-pasting from forms.

  • WhenSomeone fills in your website or Facebook lead form
  • ThenLead is added to your CRM or Google Sheet, tagged by source
  • ThenA personalised welcome email goes out within minutes
  • ThenYou get a Slack or email alert with the lead details
  • ThenA follow-up reminder is created if there's no reply in 3 days

Result: no lead sits unanswered, and you can see exactly where each one came from.

Client onboarding

New clients get a polished, consistent welcome without you lifting a finger.

  • WhenA contract is signed or the first payment comes in
  • ThenA client folder is created from your template in Google Drive
  • ThenWelcome email and intake form are sent automatically
  • ThenProject tasks are set up in Trello, Asana or Notion
  • ThenA kickoff call link is sent so they can book a time

Result: every client starts the same professional way, and nothing gets forgotten.

Invoices & payment reminders

Stop chasing payments by hand and always know who has paid.

  • WhenAn invoice is created or reaches its due date

Your inbox sorts itself, and scheduling happens without the back-and-forth.

FAQ

Good to know

What kinds of tasks can you automate?

Anything that follows the same steps every time: lead follow-ups, client onboarding, invoice reminders, data entry from forms and emails, file organization, meeting scheduling, and weekly reports. If you're copy-pasting it, it can probably be automated.

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. I start with the tools you already use, like Google Workspace, your CRM and your email. If a connector tool such as Zapier or Make is needed, I'll recommend the cheapest plan that fits before you spend anything.

What if an automation breaks?

I monitor the automations I build and fix issues as part of ongoing support. Every automation also comes with a short written guide, so you're never locked in or left guessing how it works.

What hours do you work?

Any shift works for me. I work with international clients and can match your business hours, whether you're in the US, UK, Europe, Australia or Asia. We'll agree on the exact schedule during onboarding.

Do you work with a minimum commitment?

No. You can start with a free trial task to see my work first, then choose hourly or a monthly retainer. No long lock-in.

How do we communicate day-to-day? Will language be a problem?

Not at all. I work with clients from different countries and communicate clearly in English, in writing and on calls. Day to day, I usually use Slack or email for updates, with a short weekly check-in call, and I'm happy to adapt to what you already use.

Can you handle sensitive or financial data?

Yes — I follow standard confidentiality practices and I'm comfortable signing an NDA before we start.

What industries have you supported?

Through my freelance work: mainly e-commerce, coaching and consulting, and real estate — but the core skill set transfers well beyond that.
